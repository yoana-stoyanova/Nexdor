# 🏘️ Nexdor

**Nexdor** is a community-focused website designed to strengthen the bonds between people living in the same apartment block or building.

It provides:

* 💬 A **messaging system** for direct communication with neighbors.
* 📢 An **announcement board** visible to all inhabitants.

## ✨ Features

### 1. 🏗️ Create a Block

When you create a block, you automatically become its **Manager**. You can set:

* **Block ID** – unique and permanent.
* **Block Password** – for managing block settings (keep private).
* **Block Key** – shared with inhabitants to let them join the block.
* **Number of Apartments** – total apartments in the block.

🔑 Upon creation, each apartment gets a **default password**.
Managers should share each apartment’s password with the corresponding inhabitant, who can then change it.

---

### 2. 🔓 Joining a Block

Two ways to log in:

* **Manager Login** – with the **Block Password**.
* **Inhabitant Login** – with the **Block Key**.

---

### 3. 🚪 Entering an Apartment

Apartments can be accessed:

* With the **default password** (before it’s changed).
* With a **custom password** chosen by the inhabitant.

---

### 4. 💌 Messaging Other Apartments

Send and receive **private messages** with other apartments in your block.

---

### 5. 📢 Making Announcements

Publish announcements visible to **all inhabitants** of your block.

---

### 6. 🛠️ Additional Features

* 🔍 **Search** for apartments via the search bar.
* 🏷️ **Change your apartment’s name**.
* 🔑 **Change your apartment’s password**.
